What is Rialo?

Rialo is a next-generation blockchain platform built to bridge Web3 and the real world. It lets smart contracts talk directly to real-time data payments, prices, weather, anything without needing oracles or complicated middleware. Simply put, Rialo makes blockchain apps work as smoothly as the apps you use every day.
